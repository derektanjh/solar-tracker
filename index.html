How it's calculated

Solar used directly = Solar generated − Export
Actual usage = Import + Solar generated − Export
Without-solar bill = your actual usage billed under the same ToU tariff, AFA, incentive, retail, service tax and KWTBB rules as that month's bill (this method reproduces your real bills to the sen).
NEM credits cover Energy, Capacity & Network kWh-for-kWh; AFA, retail, tax and KWTBB are worked out on full import.
