Monthly details

All energy in kWh. Import/export are from the meter section ("Maklumat Meter") on page 3 of the bill: kWh P/O (I) = import peak/off-peak, kWh P/O (E) = export. Solar generated is from the inverter app.

How it's calculated

Solar used directly = Solar generated − Export
Actual usage = Import + Solar generated − Export
Net billed kWh = Import − Export (NEM credits cover Energy, Capacity & Network charges kWh-for-kWh; AFA, retail, service tax and KWTBB are worked out on full import)
Self-use % = Solar used directly ÷ Solar generated  ·  Solar share = Solar generated ÷ Actual usage
