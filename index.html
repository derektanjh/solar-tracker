How it's calculated

Formulas behind the numbers
Solar used directly = Solar generated − Export
Actual usage = Import + Solar generated − Export
Without-solar bill = your actual usage billed under the same TNB tariff (Time-of-Use or standard), AFA, incentive, retail, service tax and KWTBB rules as that month's bill. For Time-of-Use this reproduces real bills to the sen; the standard tariff uses TNB's published rates.
Rates (from Jul 2025): Energy: Time-of-Use 28.52 sen peak / 24.43 sen off-peak, standard 27.03 sen, all +10 sen above 1,500 kWh · Capacity 4.55 sen · Network 12.85 sen · Retail RM10 (waived at 600 kWh or less).
Saved = Estimated bill without solar − Bill actually paid. This works the same for NEM (exports offset Energy, Capacity & Network kWh-for-kWh) and Solar ATAP (exports earn a credit each month that doesn't carry forward), because the bill you paid already includes the credit.
Bill covered by solar = 1 − Bill paid ÷ Estimated bill without solar
